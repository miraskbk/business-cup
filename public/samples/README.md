# Fictional sample-apartment photos

These are AI-generated example photos, created with the built-in ImageGen tool for the sample-apartment demo. They depict no real rental or inspection. Each image carries a SAMPLE label. The app marks the rental as a sample, explains its simulated dates, and labels generated letters as samples.

The original generated PNGs were encoded as JPEG at quality 85 for smaller downloads, without creative edits.

Assets:
- `countertop-move-in.jpg`
- `countertop-move-out.jpg`

The client converts these assets to JPEG and uploads copies into each visitor's private evidence folder. Fixture records are real database rows scoped to that visitor; their historical dates are fictional.

## Move-in prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: fictional sample evidence photo for a renter web app. Primary request: a candid smartphone condition-inspection photograph of a modest Oakland rental apartment kitchen countertop in good condition. Close view of a clean, undamaged pale speckled laminate counter, stainless steel sink and ordinary faucet along the left edge, white tile backsplash and plain light wood cabinets; natural window light, realistic modest apartment, no people or clutter. Landscape photo. Include the exact visible small label 'SAMPLE' in the lower right corner so the picture cannot be confused with real tenant evidence. No date, no brand, no other text. This is an AI-generated fictional demonstration photo, not evidence of any real apartment.

## Matching move-out edit prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the supplied fictional SAMPLE kitchen condition photo. Create its matching move-out inspection photograph. Preserve the exact same kitchen, sink, faucet, speckled counter material, tile backsplash, light wood cabinetry, and the undamaged countertop. The countertop is still in good condition, with no scratches, cracks, stains, or chips. Move the smartphone camera slightly to the right and vary the natural daylight slightly so it is a second snapshot of the same undamaged counter. Retain the exact readable label 'SAMPLE' in the lower right. No dates, people, brands, or additional text. This is a clearly fictional AI-generated demonstration asset.
